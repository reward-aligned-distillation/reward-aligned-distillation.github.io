02 / Method

A reward signal
for every token.

Standard on-policy distillation applies teacher supervision to every token. RAD asks how each contribution affects the student's task reward.

  1. Look ahead

    Take a temporary optimizer step to predict how distillation changes the student.

  2. Score the supervision

    Differentiate through that step to estimate each token's effect on post-update reward.

  3. Align the update

    Update token weights by projected ascent, allowing individual distillation-gradient contributions to be strengthened, suppressed, or reversed.

03 / Abstract

Reward-aligned learning
from a stronger teacher.

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards improves the reasoning capabilities of large language models, but remains computationally expensive and sample inefficient. On-policy distillation provides dense token-level supervision from a stronger teacher, yet the teacher's preference does not necessarily improve task reward.

Reward-Aligned Distillation is a bilevel framework that weights distillation signals based on their predicted effect on the student's post-update reward. It differentiates through a temporary optimizer step, then updates token weights by projected ascent. Experiments on six competition-math benchmarks and LiveCodeBench-v6 support reward-guided weighting across model families and tasks.
